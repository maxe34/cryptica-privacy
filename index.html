Cryptica — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

Cryptica is a cryptogram puzzle game for iPhone and iPad, made by Maxim Cucerenco. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your data.

Your progress stays on your device

Everything Cryptica records about you — solved levels, stars, streaks, hint tokens and which puzzle you are part-way through — is stored only in the app’s local files on your device. Progress is kept separately for each puzzle language. Cryptica has no account of its own, no sign-in of its own and no server of its own, so the developer cannot see how you are playing. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What Cryptica itself collects

Nothing. The app contains no analytics service and sends no data of its own to the developer or to anyone else.

Game Center

Cryptica uses Apple’s Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. If you are signed in to Game Center, your best scores and achievement progress are sent to Apple and appear under your Game Center nickname — not your name and not your email address. That is an Apple account, not one belonging to Cryptica: the developer never sees your Apple Account details and cannot sign you in or out. iOS asks once, on first launch; declining leaves the whole game playable, and you can turn Game Center off at any time in Settings → Game Center. What Apple does with that data is covered by Apple’s own privacy policy. Your puzzle progress is not sent to Game Center and is not synced between devices.

Advertising

Cryptica shows banner ads, and rewarded videos that play only when you choose to tap for one. There are no interstitial ads anywhere in the app. Both are served by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK).

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device identifiers, the ads you have seen or tapped, and crash and performance diagnostics. Google describes this in its privacy and terms and lists the companies it may work with in its advertising partners documentation.

Cryptica does not present the App Tracking Transparency prompt, so iOS does not give advertisers your device’s advertising identifier. You can review what apps may request under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and you can limit ad personalisation under Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

Buying Cryptica Pro removes all advertising, and with it the data collection described in this section.

Purchases

Cryptica Pro and the hint pack are sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase system. Apple handles the payment; the developer never sees or stores your payment details. The app asks Apple only whether a purchase is active, so that Pro can be restored on your other devices.

Children

Cryptica is rated 4+ and contains nothing unsuitable for children, but it is not designed for them specifically and is not part of Apple’s Kids Category. It shows advertising, as described above.

The quotations in the game

Every puzzle is a passage that is in the public domain or a traditional proverb, and the rights holder is recorded for each one. This is about the content, not about you — no information about you is involved in it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — a new advertising partner, or analytics of any kind — this page is updated and the effective date above changes with it.
